# Trade Assistant — Audit of the September changes

**Date:** 1 October 2026
**Scope:** everything committed between 7 and 18 September 2026 (commits `f7bea64` to `fa0f7bf`): the new strategies, the config changes, the order-routing change, the backtest scripts, the result files and the investor material.
**Unlike the August audit, the faults below were fixed**, except where a section says the decision is yours.

---

## Verdict up front

The app could not start. One script edited `config.yaml` with a find-and-replace and left it unreadable, so from 13 September nothing ran: no dashboard, no scans, no paper trading.

Nothing was lost and nothing leaked. No order has ever been sent to the broker, no API key is in the repository or its history, and the paper book was not touched.

The strategies added in September did not work as built, and the investor deck counted them as if they did. That is the part that matters most outside this folder.

Test suite before: 30 failing. After: 1,076 passing.

---

## Fixed

### 1 · `config.yaml` was unreadable, so the app could not start
`patch_config.py` put a `#` in front of `strategy_priority:` but not in front of the twelve lines under it. That is not valid YAML. Restored to the last working version.

The same script set **every** `stop_atr` to 2.0, every `reward_multiple` to 1.5, every `max_vol_pct` to 80 and every `regimes` list to all four regimes, and switched the composite on. Those were restored too, for three reasons:

- They never ran. The file broke in the same edit, so no result anywhere used them.
- They contradicted the notes beside them. `low_beta` is the low-volatility strategy and was told to accept 80% volatility.
- They were aimed at the wrong cause. Most strategies showed zero trades on the CSV data because that data set has ten instruments, and a strategy that ranks an instrument against a universe has no universe there. Looser stops do not change that.

### 2 · The four new daily strategies crashed on every signal
`pead_drift`, `qmj_factor`, `macro_sector_rotation` and `activist_13d_tracking` each referred to a setting that does not exist (`self.params`). The app is built so that one broken strategy cannot take down a scan, so the error was swallowed and the only symptom was zero trades. They now run.

Two did not do what their description said:

- **Macro sector rotation** said it buys when the index is risk-on. It never looked at the index. It bought every instrument on every bar. It now checks the index.
- **Activist 13D tracking** needed an RSI column that only the backtest script supplied, so in the app it could never fire. It now calculates RSI itself.

None of the four has the data its name implies: there are no earnings dates, no company fundamentals, no macro data and no 13D filings anywhere in this system. Each uses a price pattern as a stand-in. Their labels now say "(price proxy)" and their descriptions say what they read.

**All four are switched off in `config.yaml`.** Before the fix they were effectively off, because they crashed. Leaving them off means fixing the crash does not change what the paper book holds.

### 3 · Nine of the twelve new intraday strategies were empty
Their code was a single line: return nothing. They were still registered, enabled and counted. They cover things this system has no data for (Level 2 order book, options flow, a news-sentiment model, closing-auction feeds). They are now a list called `PLANNED` in `assistant/strategies/intraday.py`, with what each one is waiting for.

The other three contain real rules, and their names oversell them:

| Name | What the code does |
|---|---|
| `kalman_filter` | Buys while price is above a slow average of the session. True on about a third of all bars. |
| `ou_process_reversion` | Buys when price is two standard deviations below the session average. No Ornstein-Uhlenbeck model is fitted. |
| `hmm_regime_breakout` | Buys when recent volatility jumps. No hidden Markov model. It buys whichever way price is moving, so it buys into falls. |

All three were set to trade in the intraday paper book by default, because an intraday rule with no entry in the config counts as on. **They are now switched off.** The third had no check for time left in the session and could open a position minutes before the close; that is fixed.

### 4 · An automatic order path had been added
`auto_process_idea` ran on every idea the app analysed. It approved and rejected ideas with no human involved, and with order entry switched on it would have sent orders by typing the confirmation itself.

One of its triggers was "Kelly fraction above zero". The only Kelly number in the system was calculated from a win rate of 55% that was typed into the code, not measured, so that trigger was always true.

It never fired. No idea has been created since it was added and the orders table is empty.

It is now behind a switch, `execution.auto_trade`, which is **off**. With it off the app behaves as it did in August: nothing reaches the broker without your click and your typed confirmation. If you turn it on, it trades only ideas that pass the risk gate with a confidence of 80 or more, only on a paper account, and it labels the order as auto-traded.

### 5 · The AI model names had been changed to retired models
`claude-sonnet-5` and `claude-opus-5` were replaced with two models that were withdrawn in October 2025 and January 2026. Every AI write-up would have failed and fallen back to the rule-based one. Restored.

### 6 · The backtest scripts
- `isolated_backtest.py` did not isolate anything. It moved one strategy to the top of the priority list while the others kept trading and taking the slots. It now switches every other strategy off.
- It multiplied the win rate by 100 twice, which is why the report shows win rates of 4,760%.
- It replaced ATR with a different calculation (the standard deviation of closing prices).
- `patch_config.py` and `update_strategies.py` are deleted. Running either again would have re-broken the config or overwritten the strategy fixes. `test_one.py` is deleted because the test runner picked it up and ran a full backtest every time.

### 7 · Three statements in the investor deck
- **"34 strategies from published papers"** appeared twice, beside "528 instruments, 8.94 years, 62,319 signals". That study tested 12. The other 22 names were the four daily strategies above (zero trades ever), the nine empty ones, three unmeasured intraday rules and four older intraday rules that were not part of that study. Changed back to 12, which is what the deck's own presenter notes and email templates say.
- **"The system has only been executing live trades for a few days."** No live trade has been executed. The book is a simulation on a paper account. The deck now says so.
- The results chart labelled `dual_momentum`'s numbers **"Macro Rotation"**. There is now a different strategy with that name and no results. Relabelled "Dual Momentum".

The deck file was rebuilt. Slide 11 (the ask) was not changed.

## The September strategies, measured

Each strategy run entirely on its own, on the ten instruments in `Client_Data_Export`, about nine years of daily bars.

| Strategy | Trades | Wins | Average result per trade | What to make of it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `macro_sector_rotation` | 824 | 43.9% | +0.13R | **This is the baseline, not a strategy.** It is long almost everything whenever the market is in an uptrend, so its result is what simply being invested returned over this period. |
| `qmj_factor` | 230 | 51.7% | +0.31R | The only one with enough trades to look at. It is a trend rule and behaves like the trend rules already in the library (`ts_momentum` +0.21R, `high_52w` +0.20R). Worth putting through the full study. |
| `pead_drift` | 15 | 40.0% | +0.45R | Too few trades to say anything. All of the gain is five AAPL trades and one TLT trade; the typical trade lost. |
| `activist_13d_tracking` | 4 | 75.0% | +1.93R | Four trades in nine years. Not a result. |

R is the amount risked on a trade: +0.31R means the average trade made 31% of what it risked, after modelled costs.

For comparison, the existing strategies on the same data, each alone: `ts_momentum` 415 trades, +0.21R · `high_52w` 284 trades, +0.20R · `low_beta` 389 trades, +0.12R · `band_reversion` 32 trades, -0.85R. The other eight placed no trade on this data set.

Every instrument here was picked by hand, and most of them rose over the period. A rule that buys and holds on for a few weeks makes money on this data whether or not it has any skill, which is what the baseline row shows: +0.13R for doing nothing clever. Only the gap above that baseline means anything, and with ten instruments that move together the gap is not measurable.

Strategies that rank an instrument against a universe show zero trades here because ten mixed instruments are not a universe. That is the data set, not the rule.

## What was not checked

- The four daily and three intraday additions have not been through the full 528-instrument study or the intraday cost measurement. Until they have, nothing can be said about whether they make money.
- Currency pairs in this data set are measured coarsely. Entry, stop and target prices are rounded to two decimal places everywhere in the app, which is fine for a share at 150.00 and far too coarse for EURUSD at 1.16. This is older than the September changes and was left alone.
- The `better_strategies.py` experiments (pairs trading, the random-forest model) were read but not re-run. Its second run overwrites the first one's result file, so `ml_strategy_SPY_results.csv` holds only the 15-minute run.
- The dashboard was not started. Starting it starts the scheduler, which marks and may rebalance the paper book, and that should happen when you choose.
